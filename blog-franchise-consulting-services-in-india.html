Franchise Consulting Services in India: What They Include, What They Cost and How to Choose

Franchise consulting services are professional support for two kinds of people: business owners who want to turn their brand into a franchise, and investors who want to buy the right franchise. Here is what a good consultant does, how pricing usually works and how to choose one.

What franchise consulting services include

  • Market & feasibility assessment: is the concept ready to be franchised, and where will it work?
  • Business model & unit economics: investment, margins, break-even and payback for a franchisee
  • Brand and operations systems: SOPs, manuals, training and quality standards
  • Franchise documentation: offer document, agreement framework and fee structure (with your lawyer)
  • Lead generation and screening: finding and qualifying serious franchise investors
  • Launch and ongoing support: site selection, onboarding and growth planning

For investors: help choosing a franchise

If you are buying a franchise, a consultant can shortlist brands that match your budget, location and goals, explain the real numbers, and guide you through due diligence before you commit.

What do franchise consulting services cost?

Pricing varies with the scope of work, the stage of your brand and the consultant’s model. Some charge fixed project fees, some a retainer, and some add a success-linked component. Always ask for an itemised scope so you know exactly what you are paying for, and be cautious of anyone who promises guaranteed sales.

How to choose a consultant

  • Look for a real track record and ask to speak to past clients
  • Check that they are transparent about fees and deliverables
  • Prefer practical, hands-on support over generic presentations
  • Make sure they understand your industry and target cities
  • Insist on clear, honest communication about risks
