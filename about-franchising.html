About Franchising

A proven business model — your brand, their system, shared growth.

What is franchising?

Franchising is a business arrangement where a brand owner (the franchisor) allows an investor (the franchisee) to operate under its name, systems and support in return for fees or royalties.

Anyone with the right investment capacity, business mindset, and willingness to follow a brand’s proven system can become a franchise owner.

Franchisor

Owns the brand

Franchisee

Runs the outlet
